Privacy Policy

Connect Dots (the "app") by Tetra Developers · Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: you do not need an account, we do not ask for your name or email, and we do not sell your data.

1. Information we do not collect

The app does not ask you to sign in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or exact location.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels finished, stars, coins, hints, chosen themes and settings) is saved on your own device. It is not sent to us. If you uninstall the app, this data is removed.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type, app usage and approximate location to show ads and measure how they perform. This is done by Google under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region that requires it, the app first shows a consent form. You can choose whether ads may be personalized, and you can change your choice later from the app's settings or your device's ad settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

You can remove banner and full-screen ads with an in-app purchase (see below).

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS). We do not see or store your payment card details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so we can give you the item you bought.

5. Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers named above (Google AdMob, Google Play, Apple) as needed to run ads and purchases.

7. Security

Game data stays on your device. Ads and purchases use the secure services of Google and Apple.

8. Your choices

  • Delete your game data by uninstalling the app.
  • Control ad personalization in the app's consent settings or in your device's Google or Apple ad settings.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/sid-sys/connectdots-privacy/issues.
